About

From blockbusters
to breakthroughs

I started in visual effects - building the simulations, shaders, and pipelines that make impossible things look real on screen. Now I'm applying that same systems thinking to artificial intelligence, researching how machines can autonomously generate physically plausible worlds.

Supervised by Dr. Saber Farag. The work demonstrates that the structured, pipeline-oriented thinking developed in VFX translates directly into building intelligent systems.
