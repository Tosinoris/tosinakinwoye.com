MSc Dissertation · 2025–26

"A Scene-Aware, Feedback-Driven Approach to Intelligent Video Generation Using Multi-Agent Planning"

This dissertation presents a fully algorithmic, closed-loop pipeline for physics-aware video generation. The system orchestrates multi-agent planning to decompose scenes, constructs 3D environments in Houdini, synthesises video through Wan2.1 I2V, evaluates output against six weighted quality metrics, and adaptively refines - all without human intervention.

The project demonstrates that the structured, iterative pipeline thinking developed in VFX production translates directly to building intelligent generation systems.

Supervised by Dr. Saber Farag · Northumbria University

Detailed Breakdown
01

Multi-Agent Planning

Multiple GPT-4o-mini agents collaborate to decompose a high-level scene description into structured parameters: object placement, physics properties, camera angles, lighting conditions, and temporal dynamics. Each agent specialises in a different aspect of scene understanding.

Technology
GPT-4o-mini · Multi-agent orchestration · Structured output
02

Houdini 3D Scene Generation

The planned scene parameters are translated into a full 3D environment inside Houdini. This is where VFX pipeline expertise becomes critical - procedural geometry, physically-based materials, simulation setups, camera rigs, and render passes are constructed programmatically.

Technology
Houdini · Procedural modelling · Physics simulation · Python/VEX
03

Wan2.1 I2V Video Synthesis

Rendered frames from Houdini are fed into the Wan2.1 Image-to-Video 14B model, which synthesises temporally coherent video sequences. The 3D-rendered input provides strong physical priors, giving the generative model a structurally sound foundation to work from.

Technology
Wan2.1 I2V 14B · Image-to-video diffusion · Temporal coherence
04

Automated Evaluation

Generated videos are scored against six weighted quality metrics that assess physical plausibility, visual fidelity, temporal consistency, and scene accuracy. This evaluation is entirely algorithmic - no vision-language model (VLM) is used, which is a deliberate scope decision (VLM evaluation is designated as future work).

Technology
6 weighted metrics · Automated scoring · No VLM
05

Adaptive Refinement Loop

Evaluation scores feed back into the planning stage, enabling the system to iteratively refine its approach. The loop is closed and autonomous - the system identifies weaknesses in its output and adjusts scene parameters, camera placement, or simulation settings without human direction.

Technology
Closed-loop feedback · Adaptive re-planning · Score-driven iteration
Looking Ahead

Future work includes integrating vision-language model (VLM) evaluation into the assessment stage and introducing human-in-the-loop checkpoints for creative direction. The pipeline is designed to be extensible - each stage can be independently upgraded as models and tools improve.
