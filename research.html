MSc Dissertation - 2025/26

"A Scene-Aware, Feedback-Driven Approach to Intelligent Video Generation Using Multi-Agent Planning"

A fully algorithmic, closed-loop pipeline for physics-aware video generation. The system orchestrates multi-agent planning, constructs 3D environments in Houdini, synthesises video through Wan2.1 I2V, evaluates output against six weighted metrics, and adaptively refines - all without human intervention.

Supervised by Dr. Saber Farag - Northumbria University

Fully algorithmic - no human in the loop Closed-loop adaptive refinement Physics-aware 3D scene generation Six weighted evaluation metrics
Summary

Pipeline Explainer

A walkthrough of the end-to-end system, from text prompt through multi-agent planning, Houdini scene construction, video generation, and adaptive refinement.

Layer 1

Multi-Agent Planning

Three GPT-4o-mini agents - Director, Storyboard, and Cinematographer - collaborate to decompose a scene description into structured parameters. Each agent handles a specialised aspect of scene understanding, with outputs structured through Pydantic and Chain of Thought reasoning.

Layer 2

Houdini 3D Scene Construction

Planned parameters become a full 3D environment in Houdini. Procedural geometry, physics-based materials, collision detection, depth maps, and simulation setups are constructed programmatically - the same techniques used on feature films, now driven by AI planning.

Layer 3

Video Generation - Wan2.1 I2V 14B

Rendered frames from Houdini feed into the Wan2.1 Image-to-Video 14B model at 624x352, 25 frames, 8fps. The 3D-rendered input provides strong physical priors for more plausible motion than pure text-to-video approaches.

Three test scenes were used to evaluate the pipeline across different motion types:

Scene 1 - Straight Motion
Scene 2 - Curved Motion
Scene 3 - Collision Motion
Layer 4

Automated Evaluation

Generated videos are scored against six weighted quality metrics assessing physical plausibility, visual fidelity, temporal consistency, and scene accuracy. The evaluation is entirely algorithmic - no VLM is used (designated as future work).

Layer 5

Adaptive Refinement Loop

Evaluation scores feed back into the system, closing the loop. The pipeline identifies weaknesses, adjusts generation parameters (guidance scale, motion bucket, noise augmentation, seed), and iterates until the convergence threshold of 0.75 is met or iteration limits are reached.

Detailed Breakdown
01
Multi-Agent Planning

Director, Storyboard, and Cinematographer agents generate structured scene plans via GPT-4o-mini with Chain of Thought reasoning and Pydantic outputs.

GPT-4o-mini - Multi-agent orchestration - Structured output
02
Houdini Scene Generation

Procedural 3D environments with depth maps, collision geometry, and physics simulation setups built programmatically from the agent plans.

Houdini - Procedural modelling - Physics sim - Python/VEX
03
Wan2.1 I2V Video Synthesis

Image-to-video generation at 624x352, 25 frames, 8fps with 30-step joint inference. The 3D-rendered input provides geometry, lighting, and depth priors.

Wan2.1 I2V 14B - Image-to-video diffusion - 14B parameters
04
Six-Metric Evaluation

First-frame similarity (SSIM/PSNR), reference sequence similarity, temporal consistency (optical flow), edge/structure (Canny IoU), motion alignment (flow vectors), trajectory alignment (centroid path vs Houdini ground truth).

6 weighted metrics - Total weight 1.00 - Algorithmic scoring
05
Adaptive Refinement

Closed-loop feedback identifies the weakest metric each iteration and adjusts a single generation parameter. Early stopping triggers at convergence threshold 0.75. Scene 3 Config B converged at 0.7525 on iteration 3.

Closed-loop feedback - Score-driven re-planning - Autonomous
Looking Ahead

Future work includes VLM-based semantic evaluation, AnimateDiff with ControlNet for finer motion conditioning, video-to-video refinement models, and depth map integration as a direct conditioning signal.
